ABOUT THE PLATFORM

One campus. Many interests. More possibilities.

CampusConnect is a college club and engagement platform concept designed to bring student communities, events, projects and opportunities together in one organised digital space.

Collaborate

WHY CAMPUSCONNECT?

College life is more than classes.

Students often discover opportunities through separate groups, posters and word of mouth. CampusConnect explores how a shared platform could make club information easier to find and campus participation easier to organise.

HOW IT WORKS

A clearer path to getting involved.

Explore communities

Discover activities

View event information and find activities that fit your interests.

Connect and contribute

Use the platform's planned student and coordinator features to participate and collaborate.

BUILT AROUND STUDENTS

Designed for the whole campus community.

The platform concept includes student discovery, club coordinator tools and college-level oversight. Future development can add verified accounts, membership requests, event registrations, attendance records, announcements and activity insights.
